## 1. Historical Core Definition

The Cloud-over-Cloud Control Center served as the translation and coordination
bridge for the former Legion dispatch system. It described how scouting data from
external environments could be translated into repository artifacts through a governed relay flow.

This role mapping is no longer Current architecture. Tools/models are replaceable Cloud/Carrier or execution bindings and do not own Stable Identity or Authority by name.

---

## 2. Historical Coordination Roles

- **Gemini (Scout Node):** Identified and captured external signals, evidence,
  and raw data from cloud ecosystems (e.g., Google, Slack).
- **Jules (Semantic Bridge Node):** Translated raw scout data into structured
  XuanLing semantics, applying hardening rules and ensuring alignment.
- **Codex (Repo Bridge Node):** Performed repository hygiene, structural
  verification, and registration of translated artifacts into the corpus.

Current rule: these names are implementation specimens only. Equivalent functions may be served by different tools, windows, or carriers under the same bounded authority and return contract.

---

## 4. Historical Relay Flow

1. **Capture (Scout):** Raw signal identified and captured by Gemini.
2. **Evidence Classification (Scout):** Initial classification into `Radar`,
   `Signal`, or `Pending`.
3. **Semantic Translation (Jules):** Conversion of classified data into
   durable markdown artifacts, ensuring no forbidden semantic promotions.
4. **Registry Submission (Codex):** Final writeback to `main` and update of
   `REPOSITORY_CORPUS_INDEX.md` and `UNIFIED_ARTIFACT_REGISTER.md`.

Current correction: merge/writeback/repository registration does not establish Current, receiver absorption, release approval, or authority. Those require their own state/evidence/receiver gates.

---

## 5. Historical Failure Handling

If any node failed to process its packet or violated a hardening rule:
- **Halt sequence.**
- **Route failure to AXIS-05.**
- **Log error** with `Return_Path` and `Risk_or_Blocker` fields.

Current correction: failure is localized to affected scope; fixed AXIS routing is historical. Boundary-first handling may contain only the affected branch and preserve unrelated lawful work.
