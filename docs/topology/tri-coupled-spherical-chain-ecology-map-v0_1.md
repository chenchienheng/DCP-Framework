## Core

The three repositories are not merely three storage places. In the XuanLing cell-ecology logic, they form a tri-coupled topology: Core, Flow, and Field. Each repo is a bounded carrier ring that must link to the others through Source, Carrier, Authority, Gate, Action, Return, and Rebuild.
