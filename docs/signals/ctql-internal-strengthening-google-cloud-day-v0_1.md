## Core

External AI signals should not become company reports by default. They should be absorbed into internal capabilities: permission boundaries, evidence records, return checks, small build loops, and task-level cost governance.

## Red Doors

- External Signal != Company Report.
- Vendor Demo != Internal Decision.
- Capability != Authorization.
- Workflow Claim != Evidence.
- Human-in-the-loop != Formal Review by default.
- No Evidence Record != Completed.
- No Return Check != Closed.

## Final Rule

CTQL should turn external AI signals into small internal loops that can be built, evidenced, checked, and improved.
