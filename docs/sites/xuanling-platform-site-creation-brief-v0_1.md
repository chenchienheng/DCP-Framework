## First Screen

翾靈不是控制所有系統，而是讓不同生態在交會時，仍能辨識來源、權限、行動邊界、證據與回流。

### English

XuanLing is a non-dominating coordination layer for multi-ecosystem judgment, authority, evidence, and return.

### First-Screen Supporting Line

Different tools, platforms, people, and workspaces keep their own boundaries. XuanLing governs how they meet without mixing authority, losing evidence, or breaking return paths.
